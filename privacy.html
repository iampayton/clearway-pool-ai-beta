Privacy Policy

Effective July 15, 2026 · ClearWay Pool AI is a product of ClearWay LLC.

What we collect

  • Signup info: name, email and/or phone, and what you tell us about your pool problem.
  • Pool data: pool type/size, equipment details, test readings, notes, and visit history you enter.
  • Photos: test strip, pool, and equipment photos you choose to upload.
  • Technical basics: device/browser info and usage needed to run and secure the Service.

How we use it

  • To provide the Service: turn your readings into next-step cards, timers, reminders, and history.
  • To contact you about beta access and follow-ups you requested.
  • To improve the product — including reviewing photo reads and corrections to make readings more accurate.

AI processing

Photos and some entered data are processed by AI service providers (e.g., Anthropic) to interpret readings. Dosing calculations come from our own deterministic engine, not free-form AI. AI interpretations are always shown for your confirmation.

Where it lives

Data is stored with our hosting providers (including Supabase) using access controls that isolate your data from other households and companies. Form submissions are delivered via FormSubmit to our team inbox.

What we don't do

We don't sell your personal information. We don't share it except with the service providers above (who process it only for us) or if required by law.

Retention & deletion

We keep data while your account/beta participation is active. Email payton@clearwayhomesolution.com to request deletion of your data and we'll handle it.

Children

The Service is intended for adults. Pool chemical handling is for adults; keep chemicals away from children.

Changes & contact

Updates will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: payton@clearwayhomesolution.com.
